# Davion AI — Brand & Logo Guidelines (Axial Quadrant)

## 1. Logo Concept
**Axial Quadrant** represents a cranial MRI multi-planar axial field-of-view divided into four balanced quadrants around a central detection aperture, with the upper-right quadrant elevated to signify automated lesion localization.

## 2. Master Files (`assets/brand/`)
- **Primary Symbol (Light surface):** `davion-symbol-color.svg`
- **Primary Symbol (Dark surface):** `davion-symbol-dark.svg`
- **Small-Size / Favicon Cut (< 24 px):** `davion-symbol-small.svg`
- **Horizontal Lockup:** `davion-horizontal-color.svg`
- **Stacked Lockup:** `davion-stacked-color.svg`
- **Delivery Variants:** `exports/` (`-black`, `-white`, `-mono-0284C7`, `-square`, `-favicon`, `-app-icon`)

## 3. Colour Palette
| Role | Light Surface | Dark Surface | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Slate** | `#0F172A` | `#F8FAFC` | Base 3 quadrants + `DAVION` wordmark |
| **Clinical Sky Accent** | `#0284C7` | `#38BDF8` | Elevated upper-right ROI quadrant + `AI` badge |
| **Radiology Canvas** | `#06090F` | `#06090F` | App icon tile & MRI viewport background |

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Size
- **Clear Space:** Maintain at least `0.25x` symbol width (`64` units on a `256` canvas) of unobstructed padding around all four sides.
- **Minimum Size:** Digital `16 px` (use `davion-symbol-small.svg` / `favicon.ico` below `24 px`); Print `8 mm` width.

## 5. Misuse Rules
- Do not rotate, skew, stretch, or add drop shadows/glows to the vector mark.
- Do not change the offset direction of the upper-right ROI quadrant.
- Never place dark `#0F172A` marks on `#06090F` backgrounds; use `davion-symbol-dark.svg` or `exports/davion-symbol-white.svg`.
